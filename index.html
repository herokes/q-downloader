Everything a download needs, in one list

One window, one list. Status is always written in words, so you never have to guess what a row is doing.

  • Faster downloads

    Big files arrive sooner, and a slow or busy server does not stop them.

  • Videos, your way

    Paste a video link, pick the quality or audio only, and save it. Everything needed is built in.

  • A button in your browser

    Add the extension and download straight from a web page. A small window lets you check the name and folder first.

  • Torrents too

    Add a torrent, choose just the files you want, and share them back afterwards. You can make your own torrent as well.

  • Keeps going

    Close the window and downloads carry on in the background. Your list is still there after a restart, and a stopped download can continue.

  • Updates itself

    The app tells you when a new version is ready. Your settings and list stay as they are.

Three steps

  1. 1

    Paste a link

    Press Add and paste a link. Or just drop a link or a file on the window.

  2. 2

    Choose what you want

    For a video, pick the quality. For a torrent, tick the files. Pick where to save.

  3. 3

    Watch it finish

    Each row shows its state, speed and size. Pause, resume or open the file from the row.

Questions

Do I need to install anything else?+−

No. Everything the app needs comes with it.

Which videos can it save?+−

Most popular video sites. Videos that are copy-protected or need you to sign in cannot be saved.

How do I add the browser extension?+−

The app shows you the steps the first time you open it.

What happens when I close the window?+−

Downloads keep going in the background. Use the icon near your clock or menu bar to show the window again or to quit. The app can start when you sign in, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Does it delete my files?+−

Only when you ask. Deleting a download's file sends it to the Trash or Recycle Bin, so you can get it back.
